universalvideoplayer lua scripts
===============

The lua scripts in this folder can be loaded on a one-time basis by
adding the option

    --script=/path/to/script.lua

to universalvideoplayer's command line.

Where appropriate, they may also be placed in ~/.config/universalvideoplayer/scripts/ from
where they will be automatically loaded when universalvideoplayer starts.

This is only a small selection of internally maintained scripts. Some of them
are just for testing universalvideoplayer internals, or serve as examples. An extensive
user-edited list of 3rd party scripts is available here:

    https://github.com/yashhasan570/Universal Video Player/wiki/User-Scripts

(Anyone can add their own scripts to that list.)
